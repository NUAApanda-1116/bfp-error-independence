Manuscript package for arXiv / SIAM submission.
(Contents are also listed, with SHA-256 checksums, in SHA256SUMS.txt.)

Compile:
  pdflatex paper && bibtex paper && pdflatex paper && pdflatex paper

Compile the two-column layout:
  pdflatex paper-twocolumn && bibtex paper-twocolumn && pdflatex paper-twocolumn
  && pdflatex paper-twocolumn

Contents:
  paper.tex            manuscript source (SIAM class, review option;
                       10pt one-column body, 23 pages total)
  paper.pdf            compiled one-column manuscript, 23 pages
  paper-twocolumn.tex  the same source with the "twocolumn" class option added
  paper-twocolumn.pdf  compiled two-column version, identical content, 22 pages
  references.bib       28 entries, all with DOI or arXiv identifier
  siamart251216.cls    SIAM article class
  siamplain.bst        SIAM bibliography style
  figures/             the figures used by the manuscript
                       (fig_biasvar.pdf, fig3_inflation.pdf, fig_srmodes.pdf)
                       plus the five single-panel figures they were drawn from
                       (fig1 ... fig5), kept as source material
  scripts/             reproduction package, including the archived terminal
                       outputs of every batch of runs (see scripts/README.md
                       and scripts/NOTES.zh.md)
  refs/                23 cited works as third-party PDFs (author copies or
                       arXiv versions), used only for checking the manuscript
                       against the primary sources; not part of the
                       reproduction package
  COMPILE.txt          this file

paper-twocolumn.tex is paper.tex with "twocolumn" added to the \documentclass
options; keep the two files in sync when editing the text (the header of
paper-twocolumn.tex shows the one-line command that regenerates it).

Regenerate the two compound figures from their hard-coded data (no experiment
is re-run; this is what produced figures/fig_biasvar.pdf and fig_srmodes.pdf):
  python3 scripts/src/make_figures_compact.py figures

Reproduction code is in scripts/. Verify that the archived terminal outputs
contain the numbers printed in the paper tables with:
  python3 scripts/tools/check_paper_numbers.py

Every entry in references.bib carries a DOI or arXiv identifier and has been
checked against the primary source. The 23 third-party PDFs in refs/ are local
working copies and are not redistributed with the arXiv source.
